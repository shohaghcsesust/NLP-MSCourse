This folder contains the exact input sentences used for the Text-to-Speech (TTS) practical experiment.

S1_English_Short.txt  - English, short sentence
S2_Bangla_Short.txt   - Bangla, short sentence
S3_Hindi_Short.txt    - Hindi, short sentence
S4_English_Long.txt   - English, long sentence
S5_Bangla_Long.txt    - Bangla, long sentence

Two supplementary variants of S1 (slow speaking rate, alternate accent/voice) were also
synthesized to study rate/accent sensitivity; they reuse the S1_English_Short.txt text
and are not duplicated as separate input files.
